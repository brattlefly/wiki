# Entities & Legal

## Structure

BrattleFly operates as a PropCo/OpCo split:

- **BrattleFly LLC** — the operating entity. Runs the shop, guide service, retail, and events at 45 Linden Street, Brattleboro, VT.
- **Son of Jud LLC** — owns the building at 45 Linden Street. Leases space to BrattleFly LLC.

The two entities keep separate books, bank accounts, and paper trails. Inter-entity rent is formalized in a written lease, not an informal arrangement.

Zoning: MU (mixed use) zoning has been confirmed compatible with the intended use.

## BrattleFly Media

Separate production arm of the business. Jeff Diehl is the producer. Not a separate legal entity — operates under BrattleFly LLC.
