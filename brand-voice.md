# Brand & Voice

## Standing rules (non-negotiable)

- Always write **"BrattleFly"** with a capital F. Fixed by the Vermont Secretary of State filing.
- **Trico Unlimited / Battenkill** content and bookings must never be conflated with **BrattleFly's own Connecticut River / West River** guided trips. Keep the two brands clearly attributed at all times.
- **Specific fishing locations are never published online.** Spot intel is shared in person at the shop only. This is both a conservation policy and a foot-traffic driver — content teaches water-reading skills, not coordinates.
- Jeff Diehl is the media producer for BrattleFly Media. Do not assign production roles to Henry Thurber (he's sales/media support at Diamondback, not a BrattleFly Media collaborator).
- **Human review gate:** nothing AI-assisted publishes without direct review first — this applies to the website, email templates, and any public-facing content.

## Positioning

BrattleFly is built around **warm-water species** — smallmouth bass, northern pike, common carp, American shad — on the Connecticut River corridor and West River. This is the strategic differentiator in a trout-dominant regional market. July–August peak smallmouth and carp season aligns with Vermont's highest visitor volume, and there's no direct warm-water fly fishing competitor in the Brattleboro corridor.

Battenkill trout guiding is offered through the Trico Unlimited partnership (Brew Moscarello) — a distinct brand, distinct water, distinct booking path.

## Mission

Community-first: a third space for anglers of all levels, with emphasis on youth, novices, and local residents discovering their home waters. Retail is a service layer, not the primary engine — guided trips, instruction, fly consumables, and media are the real margin centers.

## Voice

Copy reads like regional outdoor writing: specific, earned, direct. No marketing gloss, no superlatives, no promotional language. Applies to all BrattleFly and Trico content.
